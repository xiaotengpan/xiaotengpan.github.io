I am a Ph.D. student at the [College of Software](https://cs.nankai.edu.cn/), [Nankai University](https://www.nankai.edu.cn/). My research interests lie broadly in **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**, **Human-AI Interaction**, and **Ubiquitous Computing**. I am particularly interested in understanding how intelligent systems can support human decision-making, learning, and safety in real-world contexts.

My current research focuses on **AI-mediated safety education and intervention**, **anti-scam technologies**, and **intelligent user experience**. I am interested in combining user studies, interactive system design, and AI techniques to understand human behavior and develop human-centered intelligent systems.

I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Nan Gao](https://nancygao.com/) at Nankai University.
