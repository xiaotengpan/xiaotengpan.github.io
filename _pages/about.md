# 🔬 Research

### Interactive Anti-Scam Training

I study how people recognize and respond to potential scams when the risk is still uncertain. My current work explores **interactive and AI-mediated simulations** that allow users to experience realistic scam scenarios, make decisions, recover from mistakes, and practice protective behaviors such as verification, help-seeking, refusal, and disengagement.

This research combines qualitative user studies, interaction design, prototyping, and empirical evaluation to understand how anti-scam education can move beyond simply teaching users to identify whether a message is fraudulent.

### Human-Centered AI for Safety

I am interested in how **Human-Centered AI** can support safety-related decision-making and education in everyday environments. Current projects explore AI-assisted safety information services, interactive safety education, campus risk communication, and intelligent systems that help users understand and respond to potential risks.

My broader goal is to investigate how AI systems can provide useful assistance while preserving human judgment, agency, and trust.

### User Experience and Ubiquitous Computing

I also work on methods for understanding user experience at scale. One line of my research investigates how large-scale online user feedback can be transformed into structured and interpretable representations of smartphone user experience.

I am interested in combining computational methods with human-centered evaluation to understand user experience across devices, platforms, and everyday computing contexts.
